Privacy

What this site keeps about you, why, and how to have it deleted.

This site is run by Noah Collin, a student at the CUNY School of Professional Studies, for a capstone project. It is for adults (18 and over).

What we keep

You can read and listen to everything on this site without an account. If you make one, we keep:

  • Your email address, only for signing in and password resets. It is held by Google Firebase Authentication. It is never copied into the ratings, never published and never shared.
  • A display name you choose. Only you see it. Please don't use your real name.
  • Your ratings and corrections: for each clip, the boxes you ticked, who you thought got it right, any corrected transcript or comment you typed, which clip lengths you played, and when you saved it. They are linked to a random user id that Firebase makes, not to your email.

Passwords are handled by Firebase, and we never see them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The sign-in keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. Our hosts (Cloudflare for the pages, Google Firebase for accounts) keep standard server logs. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts and the sign-in code from Google's servers (gstatic.com), so Google also sees your IP address when you visit, as it does on most websites.

Why

To check, by ear, the labels an AI judge gave to speech-recognition errors. Ratings may be analysed and published for research, without your name or email. See the contributor terms for the licence they are shared under.

Deleting your account and ratings

On your account page, "Delete my account and ratings" removes every rating you saved, your profile and your account, at once. You can also write to us and ask us to do it. Ratings already published in a research data set before you ask cannot be taken back from copies others have downloaded. We will delete all email addresses when the study ends, by [date not set yet].
