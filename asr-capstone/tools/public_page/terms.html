Contributor terms

What happens to the ratings and corrections you submit.

In plain words

  • Your answers may be published for research, linked only to a random id, never to your name or email.
  • They carry the licence of the recording they are about, because a correction of a transcript is built from that transcript:
    • CORAAL: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, for non-commercial use only.
    • EdAcc and Earnings-22: CC BY-SA 4.0.
    • Supreme Court: CC BY 4.0.
  • CORAAL's material and EdAcc's are kept apart: any published file of ratings has one file per data set, because the two licences don't allow them to be mixed into one work.
  • Please don't type in names or places that CORAAL has bleeped, or anything else that identifies a person.
  • Please type only what you hear. Don't paste in other people's transcripts.
  • You can delete your account and your ratings at any time (see privacy).
  • The site is free and has no ads or paid parts. The clips stay freely downloadable files; an account is needed only to save ratings.
