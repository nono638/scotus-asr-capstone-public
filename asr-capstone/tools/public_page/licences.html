Supreme Court of the United States

Oral argument audio and transcripts, October Terms 2010, 2017, 2021 and 2025. Recordings by the Court; transcripts by Alderson Reporting Company (to 2016) and Heritage Reporting Corporation (2017 on). Source: supremecourt.gov. Works of the U.S. government are not under copyright, and the Court states no licence. Changes: clipped and re-encoded.

Ramon Sanabria, Nina Markl, Andrea Carmantini, Ondrej Klejch, Peter Bell and Nikolay Bogoychev (2025), University of Edinburgh. Source: Edinburgh DataShare, doi:10.7488/ds/7914. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: audio clipped and re-encoded; reference text shown beside the engines' text. The clips, the reference text and the engines' transcripts of this audio are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

CORAAL, the Corpus of Regional African American Language

Tyler Kendall and Charlie Farrington (2023), version 2023.06, The Online Resources for African American Language Project, University of Oregon, with each component's own authors (listed on the data page). Source: doi:10.7264/1ad5-6t35. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, for non-commercial use only. Changes: audio clipped or kept whole and re-encoded; reference text shown beside the engines' text. Earnings-22
