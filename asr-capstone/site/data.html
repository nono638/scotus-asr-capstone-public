The data

Everything behind the site, in tables you can search, filter and download. Pick a table, type to search every column, and narrow it down column by column.

One row per case the failure searches list: meaning changes, invented words, edits, skips and breakdowns, plus the spots where every engine differs from the reference. Not every case found is a row: about 80,000 restarts an engine tidied away (a speaker's false starts) are counted in the rates but not listed one by one, so the edits rows are CORAAL's rewritten grammar only. The AI judge's answers (Claude Sonnet, which read every listed case) are in the last columns. A second AI judge, Qwen 3.8, is still reading the cases, so its answers are left out until it finishes. Download the whole file (CSV).

The most serious rows have a Play button with about 15 seconds of the recording around the error: those Sonnet called opposite or gave a severity of 3 or 4. Nobody has checked these by ear yet. Earnings-22 has no clips, because its audio has no stated licence. Supreme Court audio is public domain; EdAcc's is CC BY-SA 4.0 and CORAAL's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
