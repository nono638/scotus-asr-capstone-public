Five ways it goes wrong

Not the dull ones. Every engine spells some names its own way, leaves out speaker labels and writes "Court" in lower case; those count in the error rates, but they say little about how speech recognition fails. These do. The rates are what the rules found per hour, averaged over Qwen3, Whisper and Parakeet, before any judge has looked.
